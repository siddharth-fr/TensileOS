# TensileOS Community Guidelines

Welcome to the TensileOS community! TensileOS is dedicated to providing an open, welcoming, and collaborative space for retro computing enthusiasts, makers, and embedded firmware developers.

## Community Principles

1. **Mutual Respect**: Treat all contributors and community members with dignity, courtesy, and patience. Keep debates focused on technical choices, architecture, and ideas.
2. **Supportive Learning**: Embedded development and ESP32 programming span diverse backgrounds and skill levels. Encourage curiosity, answer questions generously, and share knowledge.
3. **Constructive Collaboration**: When reviewing code or discussing pull requests, offer actionable feedback, suggest solutions, and highlight positive contributions.
4. **Safety & Inclusion**: We do not tolerate harassment, hostility, personal insults, discriminatory language, or exclusionary behavior in any project spaces.

## Guidance & Moderation

Project maintainers strive to maintain a healthy and productive environment for all participants. Maintainers reserve the right to moderate discussions, hide offensive remarks, or restrict repository access if behavior violates these principles.
